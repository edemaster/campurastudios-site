Coming to Steam

PC · Windows · Linux & Steam Deck planned

A club, not a course

Golf games let you design the course. This one makes you run the club. Shape the land, lay out the holes, then keep the whole place standing while your golfers decide what they think of it.

Build it

Clubhouse, pro shop, restaurant, driving range, carthouse. Each is a real facility with real capacity and a real queue.

Staff it

Receptionists, greenkeepers, marshals and cooks, all on wages. Leave reception empty and watch what it does to your morning.

Price it

Green fees, the pro shop, the kitchen, cart hire. Every price moves who turns up tomorrow.

Every golfer is a person

Nobody on your course is a number. Each golfer arrives with a wallet, needs and an opinion of your club. They queue, buy, eat, play badly and take it personally. When they leave, they remember, and regulars become members, or don't.

Things go wrong

Nothing is scripted. Every incident starts from something your club made true, and every one can be headed off if you saw it coming.

  • Queue mutinyThe line got too long. The back of it left.
  • Slow-play rowA fourball holds up the hole behind.
  • HangryA hungry golfer with nowhere to eat.
  • Cart in the pondExactly what it sounds like.
  • Geese steal a ballThe geese were here first.
  • A proposal on the greenNot every incident is bad news.

Twelve clubs to save

Start a municipal course from nothing, rescue a club in receivership, survive a dry summer or climb into the national top 100. Every scenario has medals to earn, and there is a free-build sandbox when you just want to build.
